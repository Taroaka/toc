# Requirements

Scene creation must connect every scene after the opening to the preceding scene with a concrete reason (「なぜなら」), rather than temporal succession (「そしてそれから」). Explain the actual generation order. Preserve research truth and existing concurrent execution. Do not attribute an unverified quotation.

Success: Architect, single/batch Scene Author, and repair prompts carry the same causal rule; each plan carries the reason into isolated author turns; canonical guidance/template explain the rule and first-scene exception.
