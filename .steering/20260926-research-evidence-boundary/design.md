# Design

Update canonical guides and templates consumed verbatim by the existing research prompt builder. No runtime or validator schema migration is needed: the research transport accepts rich extension keys and the story registry preserves complete records.

- Add optional source-supported causal links between event IDs, with version and evidence references.
- Bind events and factual assertions to version IDs; retain a separate synopsis per researched version. The existing canonical dump is an overview with explicitly separated versions, not a silently selected hybrid.
- Extend facts with material_path (JSON Pointer), evidence_kind, evidence_refs, and variant_ids so individual motives/settings/interpretations can be qualified without assigning one blanket status to a character or symbol.
- Classify unresolved issues by whether research, a p200 decision, or no action is needed.
- Keep adaptation proposals explicitly labeled and optional. Do not require p100 to design growth arcs, suspense, scenes, or cinematic setups/payoffs.
- New evidence fields are authoring instructions, not new hard validation gates. Verify their examples and references, existing structural checks, grounding inclusion, and registry preservation; do not claim semantic truth is mechanically validated.
