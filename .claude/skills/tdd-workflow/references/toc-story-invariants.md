# ToC Story Invariants

When changing the research-to-story production mapping:

- Search production code, workflow, skills, verifiers, and tests for branches or fixed values tied to one title, character, location, object, or scene ID.
- Reject title checks, story-key checks, dedicated story profiles, fixed story scene/event/role tables, and prompt reinforcement that only benefits one story.
- Confirm story-specific facts come from input research and run artifacts through generic contracts.
- For story authoring changes, confirm research events, full passage text, characters, relationships, places, world rules, facts, symbols, and conflicts survive into scene lifecycle fields instead of only appearing as detached IDs.
- Reject deterministic generic prose fallback when the Story Architect / Scene Author output is missing or invalid.
- Confirm semantic scene identity is stable across target-duration changes; duration may change cut density, not duplicate story scenes by itself.
- Keep evidence that at least two different story inputs use the same production path.
- If story-specific branching is found, remove it or generalize it before completing review.
